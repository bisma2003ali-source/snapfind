The problem

You see an item of clothing — worn by someone else, in a photo, or in a screenshot from social media — and want to know where to buy it. Often the person wearing it doesn’t know, doesn’t remember, or can’t be asked (it was a gift, thrifted, or a stranger).

Generic tools like Google Lens or Pinterest Lens aren’t built for exact-item purchase intent — they frequently surface the same listing at full price instead of a confident match or better options.

The solution

Upload a screenshot or photo — or scan an item live — and get the exact match with price and purchase details when available. No exact match? You get close visual alternatives instead of a dead end.

What makes us different

  • 🏷️ Transparent match confidence — most competitors don’t clearly distinguish exact matches from close alternatives
  • 📸 Screenshot-first flow — matches how people already save outfit inspiration, not a forced live scan
  • 🌐 Multi-network data stacking — aimed at a higher exact-match hit rate than single-network competitors

How we source results

  • 🔗 Multiple retailer data networks — we query several networks in sequence (starting with Rakuten Advertising, adding CJ/Awin next) so a gap in one network doesn’t become a gap in your results
  • 🖼️ Visual fallback — if retailer networks return nothing, a general visual search still catches a visual match, even without a guaranteed purchase link
  • 🏬 Large multi-brand retailers first — ASOS, Zalando, Farfetch, SSENSE, Nordstrom — a handful of these carry thousands of brands each
