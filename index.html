What makes SnapFind different

  • 🏷️ Transparent match confidence — exact vs. close alternative, always labeled. Most competitors don’t clearly distinguish the two.
  • 📸 Screenshot-first flow — matches how people already save outfit inspiration, instead of forcing a live scan.
  • 🌐 Multi-network data stacking — we combine several retailer data networks for a higher exact-match hit rate than single-network competitors.

How we source results

  • 🔗 Multiple retailer networks — queried in sequence (starting with Rakuten Advertising, adding CJ and Awin next), so a gap in one network never becomes a gap in your results.
  • 🖼️ Visual fallback — when retailer networks return nothing, a general visual search still catches a match, even without a guaranteed purchase link.
  • 🏬 Large multi-brand retailers first — ASOS, Zalando, Farfetch, SSENSE, Nordstrom — a handful of these carry thousands of brands each.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Is SnapFind free?

Yes — SnapFind is free for users. We earn a commission when you buy through one of our links, at no extra cost to you.

How accurate is it?

We label every result. When we’re confident it’s the same item, you get an Exact Match badge. Otherwise you get clearly labeled Close Alternatives — never a silent failure.

Do you sell the clothes?

No. We find items and send you to the retailer to buy. Think of us as your search engine for fashion, not a store.

What can I search?

Any clothing screenshot or photo — from Instagram, TikTok, a saved image, or your camera roll. A live camera scan is coming soon.

Where does SnapFind work?

We launch in the United States first, then expand to the UK and Canada based on real usage data.
