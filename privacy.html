Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2025

SnapFind ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how we handle it.

1. Information we collect

  • Waitlist email — if you join our waitlist, we store your email address to notify you when SnapFind launches.
  • Images you upload — when the search feature launches, uploaded images are processed only to find matching clothing and are not sold or shared for marketing.
  • Basic usage data — anonymous analytics (e.g., pages visited, device type) to improve the site. We may enable this later.

2. How we use your information

  • To send launch and product updates (only if you joined the waitlist).
  • To operate and improve SnapFind, including measuring search accuracy.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Affiliate links

SnapFind may contain affiliate links to retailers. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Retailers have their own privacy policies — we are not responsible for their practices.

4. Cookies

We may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and, if enabled, analytics. You can disable cookies in your browser settings.

5. Sharing your data

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data with service providers who help us run the site (e.g., hosting, email), and only to the extent needed for those services.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time by emailing snapfind@gmail.com.

7. Children

SnapFind is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact

Questions? Email us at snapfind@gmail.com.
